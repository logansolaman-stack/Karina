1. Executive Summary

A two-phase Shopify Plus engagement to launch President Luggage as a DTC brand in Japan — starting with a controlled Kubiko soft launch in Spring 2027, followed by the premium Ki Line full launch in Summer 2027.

President Luggage is returning to its origin market. Sixty years after the brand began making attaché cases in Japan, this engagement builds the digital infrastructure to re-enter as a modern DTC brand — localised for Japanese consumers, backed by a Shopify Plus platform, and validated through a phased approach that manages investment risk.

This proposal covers the full scope: Shopify Plus store build, Japan-specific localisation, payment and fulfilment integration, SEO and analytics, influencer coordination support, quantitative research support, and the Phase 2 Ki Line expansion. It also addresses the migration of President Luggage's existing Asian sites to the Shopify Plus platform.

2. Understanding the Brief

President Luggage is an established Hong Kong luggage brand with deep Japanese supply-chain roots — many of its component suppliers and craft partners are Japanese. The brand occupies the "premium quality without the mark-up" position, which aligns strongly with Japanese consumer values: exacting standards for quality, design, durability, and value.

Phase 1 — Market Test

Product: Kubiko (existing hard-shell carry-on, ~$200–250 USD)

Goal: Validate the DTC channel in Japan. Test site performance, fulfilment from China (drop-ship), returns process, price sensitivity, customer acquisition, and brand reception. Minimal marketing spend — the focus is operational signal and data.

Timeline: Channel setup Q3–Q4 2026 → Soft launch Spring 2027

Phase 2 — Full Launch

Product: Ki Line (new premium product designed for Japan, positioned above Kubiko)

Goal: Scale what Phase 1 validated. Marketing investment ramps based on real customer data. Brand awareness, influencer deepening, expanded catalogue.

Timeline: Summer 2027

Both phases ship product with a Japan-specific specification: enhanced warranty, wheelstop, replaceable wheels, consistent colourways, deliberate logo treatment, TSID by Travel Sentry on every product, and Japanese-preferred payment and returns processes.

3. Scope of Work

The engagement is structured into seven work streams covering both phases, plus the parallel Asian site migration.

Work Stream A — Shopify Plus Store Build & Configuration

  • Shopify Plus account provisioning and multi-market architecture design
  • Japan market storefront creation with dedicated domain (e.g., presidentluggage.jp or /ja subpath)
  • Store settings configuration: currency (JPY), tax, shipping zones, checkout localisation
  • Shopify Markets or Markets Pro configuration for Japan-specific pricing, duties, and tax display
  • Multi-currency and multi-language storefront setup within Shopify Plus
  • Staff accounts, permissions, and workflow configuration

Work Stream B — Theme Design & Development

  • Custom Shopify theme design aligned with Japanese aesthetic preferences (clean, minimal, detail-oriented)
  • Mobile-first responsive design (mobile commerce dominates in Japan)
  • Product page templates optimised for Japanese consumers: detailed specifications, multiple imagery angles, size guides with metric units, material callouts
  • Brand storytelling sections: heritage narrative (60-year Japan origin), craftsmanship, sustainability
  • Japan-specific UI patterns: trust badges, warranty information prominent, TSID security callout
  • Accessibility and performance optimisation (Core Web Vitals targets)

Work Stream C — Japan Localisation

  • Full Japanese language translation of storefront content (not machine translation — native-quality Japanese copywriting)
  • Product descriptions localised for Japanese market: emphasis on quality details, materials, craftsmanship process
  • Customer service templates in Japanese (order confirmation, shipping, returns, warranty)
  • Legal pages: 特定商取引法 (Tokutei Shouhou Torihiki Hou) required disclosures, privacy policy, terms of service — all in compliant Japanese
  • SEO-optimised Japanese content (keyword research for Japanese luggage/travel market)
  • Currency display in JPY with appropriate pricing psychology (e.g., ¥29,800 not $250)

Work Stream D — Payment & Fulfilment Integration

  • Payment gateways configured for Japanese consumer preferences:
    • Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Amex — JCB is critical for Japan)
    • Konbini payment (コンビニ払い) — convenience store payment via Shopify-compatible provider
    • PayPay integration (dominant mobile payment in Japan)
    • Rakuten Pay (significant market share)
    • Bank transfer (銀行振込) option
    • Cash on delivery (代金引換) evaluation
  • Fulfilment setup:
    • Drop-ship integration from China warehouse — shipping rate configuration, transit time display
    • Japan domestic returns process design and Shopify workflow configuration
    • Returns address in Japan (partner warehouse or logistics provider coordination)
    • Shipping label automation and tracking integration (Yamato Transport / Sagawa compatibility)
    • Customs documentation automation for cross-border shipments

Work Stream E — Analytics, SEO & Marketing Infrastructure

  • Google Analytics 4 setup with Japan-specific event tracking and conversion goals
  • Google Search Console configuration for Japanese domain/subdirectory
  • Japanese SEO: keyword research, meta tags, structured data (Product schema), hreflang tags
  • LINE Official Account integration (Japan's dominant messaging platform — 95M+ users)
  • Social pixel installation: Instagram, Facebook, LINE, TikTok (as relevant to influencer strategy)
  • Email marketing setup (Klaviyo or Shopify Email) with Japanese templates and automation flows
  • Review and UGC app integration (Japanese consumers rely heavily on reviews before purchase)
  • UTM and attribution framework for influencer tracking

Work Stream F — Quantitative Research & Launch Support

  • Pre-launch quantitative research survey design and deployment (brand perception, price sensitivity, category awareness among Japanese consumers)
  • Survey platform setup and respondent targeting coordination
  • Data analysis and positioning recommendations based on research findings
  • Influencer identification support: research and shortlist of Japanese travel, lifestyle, design, and sports influencers
  • Pre-launch landing page with email capture for early interest
  • Shopify pre-launch configuration (password page, coming-soon, email collection)

Work Stream G — Phase 2: Ki Line Launch & Scaling

  • Ki Line product page design and development (premium positioning, enhanced storytelling)
  • Expanded catalogue integration across Japan range
  • Marketing campaign buildout based on Phase 1 data: paid social, search, influencer activations
  • A/B testing framework for optimisation (landing pages, product pages, checkout)
  • Performance reporting dashboard and Phase 1 learnings integration
  • Customer segmentation and retention marketing activation

6. Asian Site Migration to Shopify Plus

Parallel to the Japan launch, this engagement includes migrating President Luggage's existing Asian storefronts to the Shopify Plus platform — consolidating into a unified multi-market architecture.

Migration Scope

  • Platform audit: Assess each existing Asian site's current platform, data structure, and integration dependencies
  • Data migration: Products, customers, order history, content — mapped and migrated to Shopify Plus
  • URL redirect mapping: Preserve SEO equity with comprehensive 301 redirect strategy
  • Design adaptation: Align existing market storefronts with the new brand system while respecting local market preferences
  • Payment & logistics: Configure market-specific payment gateways and fulfilment for each Asian market
  • Unified admin: Single Shopify Plus organisation with multi-market storefronts, unified inventory, shared customer data
  • Go-live coordination: Staged migration with parallel-run period to minimise disruption

Note on Migration Phasing

Migration of existing Asian sites can run in parallel with or after the Japan Phase 1 build, depending on President Luggage's priority and resource allocation. A separate migration timeline will be agreed once the Japan store architecture is established. Pricing below covers the migration as a fixed-fee engagement per market.

  • Payment gateway accounts: Client establishes merchant accounts with payment providers. Integration and configuration included in scope.
  • Fulfilment partner: Client identifies Japan logistics/returns partner. Shopify configuration and integration included in scope.
  • Influencer partnerships: Influencer fees (product gifting, paid partnerships) are client budget. Research, shortlisting, and coordination included in scope.
