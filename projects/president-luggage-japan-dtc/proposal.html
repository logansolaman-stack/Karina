Executive Summary01

A two-phase Shopify Plus engagement to re-introduce President Luggage as a DTC brand in Japan — the market where the brand began 60 years ago. Kubiko soft launch Spring 2027, Ki Line full launch Summer 2027.

This proposal covers the full scope: Shopify Plus store build, Japan-specific localisation, payment and fulfilment integration, SEO and analytics, influencer coordination support, quantitative research, and the Phase 2 Ki Line expansion. It also addresses the migration of President Luggage's existing Asian sites to the Shopify Plus platform.

Understanding the Brief02

President Luggage is an established Hong Kong luggage brand with deep Japanese supply-chain roots — many of its component suppliers and craft partners are Japanese. The brand occupies the "premium quality without the mark-up" position, which aligns strongly with Japanese consumer values: exacting standards for quality, design, durability, and value.

Phase 1 — Market Test

Product: Kubiko (existing hard-shell carry-on, ~$200–250 USD)

Goal: Validate the DTC channel in Japan. Test site performance, fulfilment from China (drop-ship), returns process, price sensitivity, customer acquisition, and brand reception. Minimal marketing spend — the focus is operational signal and data.

Timeline: Channel setup Q3–Q4 2026 → Soft launch Spring 2027

Phase 2 — Full Launch

Product: Ki Line (new premium product designed for Japan, positioned above Kubiko)

Goal: Scale what Phase 1 validated. Marketing investment ramps based on real customer data. Brand awareness, influencer deepening, expanded catalogue.

Timeline: Summer 2027

Both phases ship product with a Japan-specific specification: enhanced warranty, wheelstop, replaceable wheels, consistent colourways, deliberate logo treatment, TSID by Travel Sentry on every product, and Japanese-preferred payment and returns processes.

Scope of Work03

The engagement is structured into seven work streams covering both phases, plus the parallel Asian site migration.

A. Shopify Plus Store Build & Configuration

  • Shopify Plus account provisioning and multi-market architecture design
  • Japan market storefront creation with dedicated domain (e.g., presidentluggage.jp or /ja subpath)
  • Store settings: currency (JPY), tax, shipping zones, checkout localisation
  • Shopify Markets / Markets Pro configuration for Japan-specific pricing, duties, and tax display
  • Staff accounts, permissions, and workflow configuration

B. Theme Design & Development

  • Custom Shopify theme aligned with President Luggage brand identity — clean, minimal, detail-oriented
  • Mobile-first responsive design (mobile commerce dominates in Japan)
  • Product page templates optimised for Japanese consumers: detailed specs, multiple imagery angles, metric size guides, material callouts
  • Brand storytelling: heritage narrative (60-year Japan origin), craftsmanship, sustainability
  • Japan-specific UI patterns: trust badges, warranty information prominent, TSID security callout
  • Accessibility and Core Web Vitals optimisation

C. Japan Localisation

  • Full native Japanese copywriting (not machine translation)
  • Product descriptions localised: emphasis on quality details, materials, craftsmanship process
  • Customer service templates in Japanese (order confirmation, shipping, returns, warranty)
  • Legal pages: 特定商取引法 (Tokutei Shouhou Torihiki Hou) required disclosures, privacy policy, terms of service
  • SEO-optimised Japanese content (keyword research for Japanese luggage/travel market)
  • Currency display in JPY with appropriate pricing psychology (e.g., ¥29,800 not $250)

D. Payment & Fulfilment Integration

  • Payment gateways: Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Amex), Konbini (コンビニ払い), PayPay, Rakuten Pay, Bank transfer (銀行振込), Cash on delivery (代金引換)
  • Fulfilment: Drop-ship integration from China, Japan domestic returns process, shipping automation (Yamato/Sagawa), customs documentation automation

Asian Site Migration to Shopify Plus06

Parallel to the Japan launch, this engagement includes migrating President Luggage's existing Asian storefronts to the Shopify Plus platform — consolidating into a unified multi-market architecture.

Migration Scope

  • Platform audit: Assess each existing Asian site's current platform, data structure, and integration dependencies
  • Data migration: Products, customers, order history, content — mapped and migrated to Shopify Plus
  • URL redirect mapping: Preserve SEO equity with comprehensive 301 redirect strategy
  • Design adaptation: Align existing storefronts with the President Luggage brand system while respecting local market preferences
  • Payment & logistics: Configure market-specific payment gateways and fulfilment for each Asian market
  • Unified admin: Single Shopify Plus organisation with multi-market storefronts, unified inventory, shared customer data
  • Go-live coordination: Staged migration with parallel-run period to minimise disruption

Note on Migration Phasing

Migration of existing Asian sites can run in parallel with or after the Japan Phase 1 build, depending on President Luggage's priority and resource allocation. A separate migration timeline will be agreed once the Japan store architecture is established.

Recommended 3PL Solutions (China → Japan)11

Shipping luggage requires optimizing for volumetric weight and fragile hard-shell protection. The best 3PL options for direct DTC drop-shipping from China to Japan with native Shopify integration:

Option 1: EcommOps / Floship (China Direct DTC Fulfillment)

How it works: Warehouse in Shenzhen or Dongguan picks, packs, and ships luggage directly to Japanese end-consumers.

Shipping & Last-Mile: Ships via dedicated China-Japan direct air/express routes with direct hand-off to Sagawa Express or Yamato Transport in Japan.

Delivery Time: 4–7 business days to Tokyo/Osaka.

Best for: Phase 1 Market Test (zero fixed warehousing costs in Japan).

Option 2: Shipnavi3PL / WE Co. (Hybrid Japan Local Warehouse & Returns)

How it works: Local Japanese 3PL with a warehouse near Tokyo (Yashio) that integrates with Shopify.

Role: Acts as the Japanese domestic returns center (for Phase 1) and can store bulk inventory shipped from China for 1–2 day local delivery (for Phase 2 scaling).

Compliance Support: Provides Attorney for Customs Procedure (ACP) / Importer of Record (IOR) support for clearance into Japan.

Option 3: Yamato Global Logistics / Sagawa Direct Access

How it works: Direct cross-border ecommerce logistics service operated by Japan's leading carriers.

Best for: Smooth customs clearance, local Japanese tracking numbers generated at the China warehouse, and seamless domestic return pickup.
